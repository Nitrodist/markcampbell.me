Summary. BDPQ Automation uses QuickBooks Online data only for the company you connect, and only within the permissions you approve on Intuit’s consent screen. We do not receive your QuickBooks password. We do not sell QuickBooks data or use it for advertising. You can disconnect BDPQ Automation inside QuickBooks Online at any time, and we then delete the QuickBooks data we store, as described below.

1. Who we are

BDPQ Automation is operated by 10014171 Manitoba Ltd., a corporation existing under the laws of Manitoba (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy describes how we collect, use, share, retain, and delete information when you use BDPQ Automation, including information we receive from QuickBooks Online.

This policy applies to the BDPQ Automation application and to QuickBooks Online data that BDPQ Automation processes. It is the privacy policy for the BDPQ Automation app listing.

2. Definitions

  • QuickBooks Online means the online accounting service provided by Intuit Inc. (“Intuit”).
  • Company Data means information in a QuickBooks Online company that you authorize BDPQ Automation to access through Intuit’s application programming interfaces.
  • You means the person who uses BDPQ Automation and, where you connect a company, the business that authorized that connection.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the contents of support messages. If a paid plan is offered, our payment processor collects billing details. We do not store full payment card numbers on our own systems.

3.2 QuickBooks Online data

When you connect a QuickBooks Online company, you choose what BDPQ Automation may access on Intuit’s consent screen. We receive only the Company Data covered by the permissions you approve. Depending on those permissions and the features you use, Company Data may include:

  • Company profile information, such as legal name, address, contact details, and fiscal settings.
  • Lists stored in QuickBooks Online, such as the chart of accounts, customers, vendors, and products or services, including names and contact details on those records.
  • Transactions and documents, such as invoices, bills, estimates, sales receipts, payments, and credit memos, including amounts, dates, descriptions, tax codes, and related metadata.

If you use a feature that creates or updates records, we send the corresponding changes back to that QuickBooks Online company. We do that only to carry out the action you take or the sync you have turned on.

We do not receive your Intuit or QuickBooks password. Intuit performs the sign-in. We store the OAuth access token and refresh token that Intuit issues for the connected company so we can keep the connection until you disconnect it or it expires.

3.3 Technical information

We collect limited technical information needed to run and protect the service, such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, diagnostic logs, and security events. We do not use this information to advertise to you.

4. How we use information

We use information for these purposes:

  • To provide BDPQ Automation to the QuickBooks Online company that authorized the connection.
  • To read and write Company Data only as needed for the features you use.
  • To authenticate the connection and refresh it with Intuit.
  • To respond when you contact us for support, which may include looking at the connected company’s data to answer that request.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law, such as billing or tax records if you purchase a paid plan.

We may use aggregated or de-identified technical statistics, such as error rates, that do not identify a person or a QuickBooks company, to keep the service reliable.

5. How we do not use QuickBooks data

We use Company Data only to provide BDPQ Automation to the company that authorized access, and as otherwise described in this policy. In particular:

  • We do not sell or rent Company Data or other personal information.
  • We do not use Company Data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers or for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use Company Data to market to your customers, vendors, or employees.
  • We do not use Company Data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not allow a service provider to use Company Data for its own purposes.

6. How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Intuit. Company Data is sent to and received from Intuit because you connected QuickBooks Online. Intuit’s own privacy notice applies to Intuit’s processing.
  • Service providers. We use vendors to host the application, send email, and monitor errors. They process information only on our instructions, under confidentiality obligations, and only to operate BDPQ Automation. We will identify our current service providers on request.
  • Professional advisers. We may share information with lawyers or accountants who are bound to keep it confidential, when we need their advice.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that the law, a court, or a regulator requires it, or that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or us.
  • Business transfer. If we transfer BDPQ Automation to another operator, Company Data may transfer with it. We will notify you of the transfer. The recipient must honor this policy or a policy that is at least as protective of Company Data.
  • With your direction. We share information when you ask us to.

7. Intuit and QuickBooks Online

BDPQ Automation is an independent application. It is not owned, operated, or endorsed by Intuit. Your use of QuickBooks Online remains governed by your agreement with Intuit and by Intuit’s privacy practices.

You can review and revoke BDPQ Automation’s access at any time in QuickBooks Online, from the Apps (or connected apps) area of the company file. Revoking access stops further collection. Section 8 explains deletion.

We handle Company Data in line with the permissions you grant and with the Intuit developer terms that apply to us as the provider of BDPQ Automation.

8. Retention and deletion

While a company stays connected, we keep Company Data only for as long as we need it to provide the features you use.

When you disconnect BDPQ Automation in QuickBooks Online, or when you email us and ask us to delete a connection, we invalidate the stored OAuth tokens and delete Company Data for that company without undue delay, and in any event within 30 days. Residual copies in encrypted backups are not used for any other purpose and are overwritten in the ordinary backup cycle, which we complete within 90 days. If a backup must be restored, we delete the restored Company Data again.

We may retain limited account records after disconnection, such as your email address, connection timestamps, support correspondence, and billing records if you paid for BDPQ Automation. We keep those records where we need them for security, dispute resolution, or a legal obligation. They do not include your accounting ledger.

You may request deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing me@markcampbell.me. We may need to confirm that you are authorized to act for the QuickBooks Online company. We will not delete information we are required to keep by law.

9. Security

We protect information in transit with TLS. Access to production systems and to stored tokens is limited to what is required to operate BDPQ Automation. OAuth tokens are stored so they are not publicly accessible. We do not store QuickBooks passwords, because we never receive them.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. If we confirm a breach of security that affects your personal information or Company Data, we will notify you and Intuit where the law or our obligations to Intuit require it.

10. Your choices and rights

You may:

  • Decline or limit permissions on Intuit’s consent screen. Some features will not work without the permissions they need.
  • Disconnect BDPQ Automation from QuickBooks Online at any time.
  • Ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent to further processing by disconnecting the company and asking us to delete Company Data.

Because we operate from Canada, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) may apply to our handling of personal information. If you have a concern, contact us first. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. You, or an agent you authorize in writing, may email a request to the address below. We will verify the request before we act on it.

11. Where we process information

We operate BDPQ Automation from Canada. Company Data and account information may be processed in Canada and in other countries where our infrastructure providers maintain their systems. Those providers are required to protect the information and to use it only to provide services to us. By connecting a QuickBooks Online company, you understand that your information will be processed in those locations.

12. Children

BDPQ Automation is a business application and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to BDPQ Automation, email us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version at this same address and change the “last updated” date. If a change materially expands how we use Company Data, we will notify you in the application or by email before the change takes effect, where notice is required. Continued use of BDPQ Automation after the effective date means you accept the updated policy. If you do not agree, disconnect BDPQ Automation before the change takes effect.
